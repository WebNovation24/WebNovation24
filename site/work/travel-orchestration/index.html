Identity · authorization · RLS

Authentication and business authorization

I implemented the Better Auth foundation and Supabase bridge around a normalized ActorIdentity and server-authoritative ActorContext. Current organization and membership state is rechecked in PostgreSQL, with RLS providing a second boundary instead of trusting session state alone.

Async · retries · recovery

Provider reconciliation

I implemented SaaS billing/provider reconciliation with a durable event inbox, idempotence, stale/out-of-order rejection and bounded recovery so provider state remains a projection rather than the product’s business authority.

03 · Technical decisions

Identity, state authority and reproducible verification.

Three decisions keep authority explicit across the system.

01

Authentication is not business authorization

Problem. An authenticated session proves identity, but not organization scope or business capability.

Decision. Separate authentication/session ownership from business identity and authorization, with database RLS as defense in depth.

Verification. Repository documentation covers integration testing and pgTAP/RLS validation within the local verification gate.

02

Unknown is a first-class outcome

Problem. External operations can leave the client uncertain after a timeout, interrupted navigation or provider return.

Decision. Represent uncertain outcomes explicitly and reconcile against authoritative state before declaring success.

Verification. The invariant is explicit in the repository’s current product authority.

03

Make release evidence reproducible

Problem. A multi-package SaaS can pass isolated checks while failing at data, build or browser boundaries.

Decision. Use a local verification gate spanning static checks, database/RLS validation, build and browser E2E.

Verification. README/scripts document the gate scope and staging separation without claiming universal environmental proof.

04 · Real verification evidence

Identity can stay valid while access is revoked.

A sanitized AUTH-0 verification summary with tokens, user IDs, keys and environment identifiers removed.

Outcome

Identity, authorization and external outcomes stay separate.

A session establishes identity; current business capabilities and RLS decide access; external/provider results are reconciled before they become authoritative product state.
